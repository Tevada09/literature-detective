YOUR INVESTIGATION GUIDE

Four missions.
One great story.

Reading is more than saying the words. A good detective looks closely, thinks carefully, and asks questions.

01
🔤

Word Detective

Find unfamiliar words and use the clues around them to understand what they mean.

02
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Study the characters. What are they like? What do their actions tell you?

03
🧩

Plot Detective

Follow the events. Find the problem, the important moments, and the ending.

04
💡

Meaning Detective

Look beyond the events. What lesson or message does the story share?

A small reading tip

If you find a difficult word, don't stop immediately. Read the whole sentence and look for clues that can help you guess its meaning.
